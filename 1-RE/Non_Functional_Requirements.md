# Non-Functional Requirements — Patient Health Record Consent Management System

## NFR-01 Security
Patient health records and consent information shall be protected from unauthorized access.

## NFR-02 Authorization
Access decisions shall enforce provider verification and the patient's consent scope.

## NFR-03 Auditability
Access and consent-related activities shall be traceable through audit records.

## NFR-04 Performance
Consent validation and authorized record-access requests should be processed efficiently to support responsive healthcare workflows.

## NFR-05 Availability
The system should remain available for authorized patients and healthcare providers during normal operation.

## NFR-06 Maintainability
The architecture should separate presentation, business rules, and data access so individual parts can be maintained with limited impact on other layers.
